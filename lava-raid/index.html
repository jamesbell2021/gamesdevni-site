Lava Raid

The volcano is going off, and the mammy dinosaur has a nest full of eggs. Sneak across, nick an egg while she's asleep, and carry it back to your cave without getting splatted by the lava. Just like the wee LCD games you got in your stocking in 1982, only this one lives in your browser and never needs a new battery.

How to play

  • Press GAME A to start (GAME B is faster, for experts).
  • Walk RIGHT to the nest. If the mammy is asleep (Zzz) you grab an egg.
  • Walk LEFT back to your cave to bank it. One point an egg.
  • Lava falls in the three spaces in between. Don't be under it when it lands!
  • When she lifts her head, get out of the nest. If she's awake when you're there, she'll have you.
  • Three misses and it's game over. Reach 100 and 300 and your misses are wiped.
  • It gets faster the more eggs you steal.
