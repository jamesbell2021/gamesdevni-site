Hippo Island

Get it on itch.io - Windows, Linux and Android

A minute of play, filmed in the game, October 2026.

A family out of Portaferry, bound for the New World, goes down with their ship in a storm and swims for the shore of a small, lovely island. Palms, bananas, a spring of sweet water. It would be paradise, except that somebody else lives here. A hippo.

Hippo Island is a cute, harsh, one-screen survival game. Keep Hugh, Martha, wee Tam, Nell and Bonnie the dog fed, watered, warm and well through season after season, and keep every one of them out of Humphrey's way. He sleeps in his wallow, then he wanders, and he cannot be killed. Nobody is coming for you unless you make them see you. The island wears out. The game remembers your longest stay.

  • Gather, chop, quarry, fish and farm; craft at the bench: clothes, a lookout, a boat, a signal fire, a HELP sign.
  • Storms with lightning, hurricane winds and rogue waves. High tides. Days when a shark works the shallows.
  • The signal fire may bring a ship. It may bring pirates.
  • Lose someone and the colour drains out of the island. Only a pat for Bonnie brings it back, for a while.
  • Craft crosses for the lost, or they walk at night.
  • Built from the Forged in Formation cast and island kit; every sound and tune made in code.

Block War Belfast 2098

Play it on itch.io - name your own price, in your browser, Windows and Linux

Trailer, filmed in the game, October 2026.

Belfast, 2098. Four tower blocks stand in a row by the Lough, Samson and Goliath glowing across the water, and the blocks have had enough of each other. Knock the other blocks down. Keep yours up.

A turn-based block war for one to four players: take turns at one screen, play over your local network, or take on the computer. Roll for command points, then move your residents up the stairs, down the bin chute, across the skybridge, or swinging across the gap on a grapple, and let fly. Every hit cracks a room; anything left with nothing holding it up falls for real and smashes whatever it lands on.

  • Stone throwers, petrol bombers, snipers and RPG men.
  • Scooter bombers: a time bomb on a mobility scooter, and no stairs.
  • Local hoods, lobbing fridges and tellys out of the upstairs windows.
  • Hen parties, the hardest in a fight.
  • Fires that spread until the sprinklers win, unless someone takes out the power room.
  • Mania cards (Chip Pan Fire, Karaoke Night, Belfast Rain), then the enforcers arrive to end it.
  • A pumping chiptune soundtrack where a tin whistle and a flute band play the same tune.

Periscope Vectors

Get it on itch.io - $1 or more, for Windows and Android

Trailer, from the game's itch.io page.

Sink the convoys. Count the tonnage. Survive the depth charges. Periscope Vectors is a neon vector submarine game for your phone or PC: plot a patrol across the North Atlantic, hunt down the convoys, and make your attack through the periscope.

The torpedoes take time to run, so you have to lead your target. Then the escorts come looking for you: dive, change depth after every ping, hide under the thermal layer, and don't go below crush depth. Your score is the tonnage you sink in one patrol.

  • The patrol chart: convoys on the shipping lanes, lone merchants, and air cover near the land.
  • Freighters, tankers, coasters and their escorts, each worth its tonnage.
  • The deck gun: surface and shell lone ships to save your torpedoes.
  • The AA gun against patrol bombers, or crash dive and get deep.
  • Sail home to rearm, refuel and repair.
  • A tense synth soundtrack, sonar pings and klaxons. Generic ships, with no real navies or flags.

Vector Cascades

Get it on itch.io - name your own price, for Windows and Android

Trailer, from the game's itch.io page.

Vector Cascades: Lucky 9 is pinball, pachinko and a coin pusher in one, made for your phone. Launch glowing steel balls into the table, then watch the coins they win tumble onto the pusher. Everything it shoves over the edge is yours.

Fill the card meter and a Lucky card drops onto the bed. Push cards over the ledge: a 9 on its own, or cards that add up to exactly 9, sets off a x10 coin cascade, with fireworks, a rain of coins, and ten seconds where every coin is worth ten. Go over 9 and you bust.

  • Each ball costs a coin: hold LAUNCH, let go, and keep it alive with the flippers.
  • Bumpers, slingshots, pachinko pegs, drop targets and coin pockets, with combos up to x20.
  • Up to four balls on the table at once.
  • A silly calculator voice shouting "LUCKY NINE!"
  • A pumping arcade soundtrack that builds as you play.
  • Tap either side of the screen to flip; keyboard and mouse on PC.

Vector Volley

Get it on itch.io - name your own price, for Windows and Android

Trailer, from the game's itch.io page.

Two neon armies. One screen. Lose your commander and you lose. Vector Volley is the 2 Player Battle from Forged in Formation, remade as an over-the-top arcade game in glowing vector lines: Red against Blue on one screen, for two players or one against the computer.

Eight units a side, and they never turn, so where you stand is where you aim. Column is fast and fits the bridges, Line fires a full volley, Square stops a cavalry charge dead. Change formation late and you pay for it. Then the dead come, in Graveyard Shift: the two armies back to back, and waves of shamblers, chargers and bloaters that never end.

  • A commander in a big plumed hat, a line of muskets, bayonets, cavalry, a cannon, a sniper, a grenadier and a brawler.
  • Hold fire to aim the cannon and the grenadier: a cannon ball wipes out everyone in its ring.
  • Powder kegs that blow up everyone near them, friend or foe, and set each other off.
  • Three fields drawn at random: The Ford, The Chasm and The Sand.
  • The computer on Easy, Normal or Hard, or two players, keyboard halves or pads.
  • Shell-shock slow motion, screen shake and a pounding rave soundtrack.

Neon Mega Tank

ネオン・メガ・タンク

Get it on itch.io - Windows, Linux and Android, in English or 日本語

Trailer, filmed in the game, October 2026.

One tank. Four crew. Neon Mega Tank is a co-op tank battle for one to four players, drawn in glowing neon like an 80s arcade cabinet.

Everyone crews the same giant tank and runs about its deck between the stations: one drives, one works the twin cannon, one the machine gun, one the repair bay. Hits punch burning holes in the deck that jam the stations beside them, so somebody has to drop what they're doing and patch them. Inspired by Lovers in a Dangerous Spacetime.

Play together on your own phones over Wi-Fi: one phone hosts, the others join, and every player watches the battle on their own screen. Short of friends? The AI crew takes the empty seats, and hands one over the moment somebody joins.

  • One to four players, each on their own phone over Wi-Fi, or together on one screen with keyboards and gamepads.
  • An AI crew that drives, aims, patches the holes and repairs the hull.
  • A neon city where everything comes down: knock out a tower's ground floor and the whole column falls.
  • Riflemen, RPG troopers whose rockets you can shoot out of the sky, drones, kamikaze buggies, tanks and heavy tanks.
  • 鬼戦車 Oni Sensha, the demon tank, every fifth wave.
  • Endless waves that keep getting harder. How long can you survive?
  • An anime-style chiptune soundtrack, and enemies who shout their last words in electronic Japanese.
  • Play in English or 日本語: one button on the title menu switches every menu, gauge and banner, and each phone in a Wi-Fi game picks its own language.

Pultz

Trailer, from the game's itch.io page.

Pull back. Let fly. Smash the castle. Haul your catapult's arm back, watch the dotted arc, and let go: stones trail light across a neon night, and castles of wood, stone and glass teeter and crash down.

Castle Crusher: fell the castle's guards before its soldiers march over the hill and reach your catapult, across five castles named for the castles of Ulster (Dundrum, Carrickfergus, Dunluce, Greencastle and Killyleagh), then round again, faster. 2 Player Mode: a catapult and a castle each, turn about on one phone or computer, with the wind shifting every turn.

  • Stone, as many as you like.
  • Fire, which sets wood and roofs burning.
  • Explosive: a powder keg that throws the whole castle about.

  • Towers that teeter, beams that snap, glass that shatters.
  • Neon vector graphics and an 8-bit chiptune soundtrack. Touch, or mouse and keyboard.

Cyber Vikings

Get it on itch.io - $1 or more, for your browser, Windows, Linux and Android

Gameplay, captured in the game: the computer playing all four clans.

A small one, made for fun: a turn-based Viking strategy game drawn like an 80s vector arcade cabinet, where every longboat, catapult and castle is a line of glowing neon on black.

March your raiders over the hills, row them across the fjord and storm your rivals' castles before winter freezes the water. Inspired by Mark Lucas's Viking Raiders for the ZX Spectrum (Firebird, 1984).

  • Play in your browser, or on Windows, Linux and Android.
  • Against the computer, hot-seat for up to four, or online: a quick match, or a four-letter code to give a friend.
  • Treasure to carry home, longboats to cross the fjord, catapults, drinking horns, and a fjord that freezes from round 8.
  • An 8-bit chiptune soundtrack and ZX Spectrum-style beeper effects.

Angry Old Men

Get it on itch.io - for Windows and Android

Trailer, captured in the game, October 2026.

Four grumpy old wizards, one screen. Pass it round, take your turn, and be the last old man standing. The computer plays any seat nobody takes, so you can play alone or with three friends.

Each turn your old man does one thing: walk, cast a spell, or whack someone with his walking stick. Magic missiles curve round the rocks, fireballs set the trees alight, and when it all gets too much there's a nice cup of tea. Inspired by Julian Gollop's Lords of Chaos (1990).

  • Pass and play for up to four on one phone, tablet or PC.
  • Magic missiles, lightning, fireballs, teleport and a cup of tea.
  • Summon imps, dwarves and wraiths, and hit their weak spots: imps love fire, lightning fries a dwarf's helmet, and sticks go straight through a wraith.
  • Fire spreads from tree to tree and burns your cover to ash.
  • A new random map every game, mirrored so nobody gets a better corner.
  • Neon vector graphics, a fantasy soundtrack, and four old men shouting their spells in a silly calculator voice.

Victory By None

Get it on itch.io - for Windows and Android

Trailer, captured in the game, October 2026.

Two nations, one phone. Each player hides three missile silos and a command bunker. Then you take turns to choose your targets in secret and pass the phone across. When you've both given your orders, the phone goes down between you and you watch both salvos cross the screen together.

You can win: destroy every silo, then hunt down the enemy's command, even after it moves into a city. But every warhead has a death toll, the blast spills into the towns beside it, and when the war is over the final count runs into the billions. If both sides hold fire in the same round, the war ends.

  • Pass and play for two on one phone, or play against the computer.
  • Your silos and command are secret. Your cities are on the map for both sides to see. You can move and rename them, or keep Londinium, Lower Wobbleton and Crumpetonia.
  • Both salvos fly at once: tap your own half of the screen to burst interceptors in the path of what's coming at you.
  • Every strike reports the military sites around it, so you can hunt the silos down.
  • Lose your command and you lose a turn, and command moves into one of your cities.
  • Neon vector graphics, a calculator-voiced computer and an 80s arcade soundtrack that builds as the war goes on.
Get Victory By None on itch.io

Victory By None is a game about nuclear war. The dead are counted, never shown.
