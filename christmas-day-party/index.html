Only a few of you? 🤖 Computer players fill the empty places in the quiz, Musical Chairs and Pass the Parcel. Professor Tinsel knows everything but takes his time. Dozy Sprout answers instantly and is usually wrong.

The pub quiz

  1. Christmas Crackers: general knowledge, old telly, a Northern Ireland question, put-it-in-order and closest number.
  2. Eyes & Ears: guess the film from emoji (🏠👦😱✈️) and name the carol the telly plays.
  3. Quick-fire true or false: eight seconds each.
  4. The Family Round: everyone secretly types answers at the start ("the worst present you ever got"), then the family guesses who wrote what.
  5. The Final Question: bet some or all of your points. Get it wrong and you lose them.

Everyone gets one 🃏 Joker to double their points, and whoever's last gets a ❄️ Snowball to throw at a rival, burying their buttons in snow.

How to play

  1. Download and unzip on a Windows or Linux computer, ideally one plugged into the telly with an HDMI lead. On Windows, run ChristmasDayParty.exe. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info then Run anyway. On Linux, run ./ChristmasDayParty in the folder.
  2. Allow it on your network. The first time, Windows asks about the firewall: tick Private networks and click Allow. Without that, the phones can't connect.
  3. Put it on the telly. The game opens in your web browser by itself. Press F for full screen and click once to start the carols.
  4. Everyone joins. Get everyone on the home Wi-Fi (not the guest network) and scan the QR code with the phone camera. The first person in is the host and picks the game from their phone.

Phone won't connect? It must be on the same Wi-Fi as the computer, not on mobile data or a VPN. Leave the black window open while you play: closing it stops the game. On Linux you may need to open port 3000 (sudo ufw allow 3000/tcp).
