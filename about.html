Who's behind 3BL_Indie

Not a big company. A dad and his two lads.

3BL_Indie isn't a studio with an office and a marketing budget. It's me, James, a 53-year-old dad from Newtownards, and my twin sons, who are 13.

I've been making games since I was 9, typing them line by line into a ZX81, my first computer. I've never stopped.

Every game here starts at our kitchen table: an idea, a sketch, an argument about whether it's fun yet. The boys playtest everything and they aren't shy about telling me when something's rubbish. The voices you hear in Forged in Formation are real people from Northern Ireland.

We're small, we're local, and we make the games we'd want to play. If you enjoy one, a wishlist or a kind word really does make our day.

James & the boys, 3BL_Indie

How we make them

Forged in Formation has been my project for over two years. The idea, the formations, the story and the design were all worked out long before AI came anywhere near it. What I didn't have was a team of programmers, so recently I started using AI coding tools to help me finish what I'd started.

That's how all our games work now: modern tools help a tiny team punch above its weight. The ideas, the design, the testing and the decisions are ours, and so is every game's bit of home.
