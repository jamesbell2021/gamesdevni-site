Four lanes, a figure-of-eight and a flyover. Every lane is inside on one loop and outside on the other, so the race is fair.

The big red button

It's a proper Scalextric-style hand controller: hold POWER to go, let go to lift off. That's it.

Go into a bend too fast and your car flies off the track and tumbles across the carpet. Listen for the tyres squealing: that's your cue to lift off. Cars slow going up the flyover and run away with you coming down.

The << >> buttons hop you into the next lane to get past. Empty cars are driven by the family: Uncle Sammy, Big Cousin, Granda and Wee Sister.

No app needed: scan the code on the screen and your phone becomes the controller.

How to play

  1. Download and unzip on a Windows or Linux computer, ideally one plugged into the telly. On Windows, run CarpetGrandPrix.exe. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info then Run anyway.
  2. Allow it on your network. The first time, Windows asks about the firewall: tick Private networks and click Allow. Without that, the phones can't connect.
  3. Grab a controller. Everyone joins the home Wi-Fi (not the guest network) and scans the QR code on the screen. Type a name and tap Join the race. Younger drivers can tick Extra grip for a stronger magnet.
  4. Race! Have a practice, choose the number of laps, then anyone taps Start the race. Wait for the five red lights to go out.

Phone won't connect? Make sure it's on the same Wi-Fi and not on mobile data or a VPN. If the computer has extra network adapters (VirtualBox, Hyper-V, a VPN), press A on the lobby screen to try its other addresses.
