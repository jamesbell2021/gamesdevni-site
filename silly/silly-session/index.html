THE SILLY SESSION

An Irish music session. A silly one.

Start with the Mountains of Mourne, then She'll Be Coming Round the Mountain, then Bob Piper's own jig. Watch the wee man, then copy him: tap the three lanes (or keys A S D) as the circles land. Each round he swaps to a new instrument and the last one stays behind to keep playing, until the whole session is one tune. Turn your sound up!
